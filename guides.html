Mortgage Guides

Types of Mortgages

Fixed-Rate Mortgage: Your interest rate stays the same for the entire loan term, so your monthly principal and interest payment never changes. Common terms are 15 or 30 years.

Adjustable-Rate Mortgage (ARM): Starts with a lower fixed rate for a set period, then adjusts periodically based on market rates. Can be riskier if rates rise.

FHA Loan: Backed by the Federal Housing Administration, these allow lower down payments (as low as 3.5%) and are popular with first-time buyers.

VA Loan: Available to eligible veterans and service members, often with no down payment required and competitive rates.

The Home-Buying Process

1. Get Pre-Approved

A lender reviews your finances and tells you how much you can borrow before you start shopping for homes.

2. Find Your Home

Work with a real estate agent to find a property that fits your budget and needs.

3. Apply for the Loan

Submit a formal application with income, asset, and credit documentation.

4. Underwriting

The lender verifies your information and orders an appraisal of the property.

5. Closing

Sign the final paperwork, pay closing costs, and receive the keys to your new home.

Understanding Your Rate

Mortgage rates depend on your credit score, down payment, loan term, and overall market conditions. A higher credit score and larger down payment typically unlock lower rates. Use our mortgage calculator to see how rate changes affect your monthly payment.
